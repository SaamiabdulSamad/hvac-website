FAQ

Common questions

Does this replace my current phone system?

No. It works alongside your existing setup. When a call goes unanswered, your phone behaves exactly as it does now — except the customer immediately gets a text back from your number.

Do I need to download an app or learn new software?

No. You receive alerts via SMS to your existing phone. The dashboard is a web page you can view on any device. Nothing to install.

What HVAC issues does it detect?

The system classifies 9 categories: Safety hazards, no heat emergencies, AC outages, system replacements, frozen coils, water leaks, weak airflow, thermostat issues, and maintenance requests — each with an estimated job value.

Does it handle after-hours or high-demand periods differently?

Yes. When temperatures are extreme — above 90°F or below 32°F — the system automatically adjusts the text-back message to acknowledge the surge and flag inbound contacts as higher urgency. It's an automated setting, not something you need to manage manually.

How long does setup take?

About 30 minutes. We configure the system on your number, connect your database, and test end-to-end before going live. You don't need to do anything technical.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime via email. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your number goes back to normal immediately.
